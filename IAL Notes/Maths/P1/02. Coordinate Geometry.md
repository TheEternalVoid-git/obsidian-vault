# 02 - Coordinate Geometry

## 1. Straight Line Formulas
For two given points $A(x_1, y_1)$ and $B(x_2, y_2)$:

* **Gradient ($m$):** 
  $$m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$$
* **Distance between two points:**
  $$d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$$
* **Midpoint coordinate:**
  $$M = \left(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2}
\right)$$

### Linear Equation Forms
* Point-Gradient Form (Highly Preferred): $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$
* General Form: $ax + by + c = 0$

## 2. Parallel and Perpendicular Lines
Let line 1 have gradient $m_1$ and line 2 have gradient $m_2$:
* **Parallel lines:** $m_1 = m_2$
* **Perpendicular lines:** $m_1 	\times m_2 = -1$ (or $m_2 = -\frac{1}{m_1}$)

## 3. Intersection Points
To find where two geometric lines cross, solve their linear expressions simultaneously. If an algebraic step yields a single value, they cross once. If a line and a curve fail to yield real solutions ($\Delta < 0$), they never touch.
